# ECON-217-50-HONS: Principles of Microeconomics
**Longwood University | Fall 2026 | HONORS SECTION**

## Course Description
Analysis of the economic behavior of individual consumers and firms. Topics include supply and demand, price theory, consumer choice theory, production and cost theory, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly), and welfare economics. The honors section incorporates mathematical applications and policy analysis.

## Learning Objectives
1. Apply supply-demand framework to real markets
2. Calculate and interpret elasticity measures
3. Model consumer and producer decision-making
4. Analyze different market structures
5. Evaluate market failures and policy responses

## Key Topics
- Supply and demand; market equilibrium
- Price, income, and cross-price elasticity
- Consumer theory: utility maximization, budget constraints, indifference curves
- Production theory: short-run and long-run cost curves
- Perfect competition: profit maximization, shut-down rule
- Monopoly: pricing, deadweight loss, price discrimination
- Oligopoly: game theory, Nash equilibrium, Cournot/Bertrand
- Externalities, public goods, information asymmetries
- Factor markets and income distribution
