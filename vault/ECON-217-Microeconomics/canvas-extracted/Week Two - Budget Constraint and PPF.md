Production Possibilities Frontier (PPF)This is a model that is used to understand how an individual, firm, or a country can and does allocate resources. In any model, we must have some assumptions:
The country or the firm only produces two goods
The country or the firm is using all their resources to produce these two goods 
Everything else is held constant (Ceteris Paribus)

Opportunity Costs: cost of the best alternative forgone. Ex: producing the first 20 MRRs cost the company 10 CSPs. 
Marginal Costs: Cost of producing one more unit. 
Diminishing Marginal Returns: 
As you consume more of a product or service, the benefits or utility decrease for successive units. 
From the production point of view, each successive unit of production will cost more.
Law of increasing Opportunity Costs: If you want to produce something more and more, you have to give up the alternative at an increasing rate.
