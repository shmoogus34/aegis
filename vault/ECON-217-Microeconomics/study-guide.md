## Elasticity Calculation Practice

**Example 1**: Price rises from $4 to $5. Qty demanded falls from 100 to 80.
$$PED = \frac{(80-100)/100}{(5-4)/4} = \frac{-0.20}{0.25} = -0.80$$
→ |PED| = 0.8 → Inelastic → Revenue increases when price rises

**Example 2**: Income rises 10%. Demand for inferior good falls 5%.
$$YED = \frac{-5\%}{+10\%} = -0.5$$
→ Inferior good (YED < 0)

## Utility Maximization Example
Px = $2, Py = $4, Budget = $20
- MUx/Px = MUy/Py → (MUx)/2 = (MUy)/4 → MUx = MUy/2
- At optimum: consume where marginal utils per dollar are equal

## Cost Curves Summary
- **ATC = AFC + AVC**
- **MC = ΔTC/ΔQ**
- When MC < ATC: ATC is falling
- When MC > ATC: ATC is rising
- **MC = ATC** at minimum ATC

## Market Structure Comparison
| Feature | Perfect Competition | Monopoly | Oligopoly |

| Sellers | Many | One | Few |
| Price power | None (price taker) | Full | Significant |
| Long-run profit | Zero | Positive | Varies |
| DWL | None | Yes | Less than monopoly |
| Real examples | Agriculture | Utilities | Airlines, tech |

## Key Formulas Sheet
- PED = %ΔQd / %ΔP
- MR = P(1 - 1/|PED|) for monopolist
- Profit = TR - TC = (P - ATC) × Q
- Consumer Surplus = Area above P* below demand curve
- Producer Surplus = Area below P* above supply curve
- DWL = ½ × |ΔP| × |ΔQ|
