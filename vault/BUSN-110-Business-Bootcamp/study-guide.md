# BUSN-110 Study Guide — Student Success

## Key Concepts for Exams

### Eisenhower Matrix Quadrants
| Quadrant | Urgency | Importance | Action |
|----------|---------|------------|--------|
| Q1 | Urgent | Important | DO NOW |
| Q2 | Not Urgent | Important | SCHEDULE |
| Q3 | Urgent | Not Important | DELEGATE |
| Q4 | Not Urgent | Not Important | ELIMINATE |

### SMART Goal Acronym
- S = Specific
- M = Measurable
- A = Achievable
- R = Relevant
- T = Time-bound

### Credit Score Breakdown
- 35% Payment history
- 30% Amounts owed
- 15% Length of credit history
- 10% Credit mix
- 10% New credit

### Budget 50/30/20 Rule
- 50% Needs
- 30% Wants
- 20% Savings/debt repayment

### Study Retention Hierarchy
Passive (lowest) → Rereading → Highlighting → Note-taking → Practice testing → Teaching others (highest)
