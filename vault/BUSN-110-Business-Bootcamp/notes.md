# BUSN-110 Notes — Student Success Strategies

## Time Management

### Priority Matrix (Eisenhower Matrix)
|             | **Urgent**       | **Not Urgent**     |
|-------------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Important**   | DO FIRST (Q1)    | SCHEDULE (Q2)      |
| **Not Important** | DELEGATE (Q3)  | ELIMINATE (Q4)     |

- Q1: Crises, deadlines, emergencies → ACT NOW
- Q2: Planning, relationships, skill building → INVEST HERE
- Q3: Interruptions, some meetings → minimize
- Q4: Busywork, time-wasters → cut

### Time Blocking
- Assign every hour a task before the day begins
- Study blocks: 25-min focused intervals (Pomodoro technique)
- Schedule breaks: 5-min after each block, 20-min after 4 blocks
- Color-code: red = class, blue = study, green = personal, yellow = work

### SMART Goals
- **S**pecific: What exactly?
- **M**easurable: How will I know?
- **A**chievable: Is it realistic?
- **R**elevant: Does it matter?
- **T**ime-bound: By when?

Example: "Complete BUSN-296 Test 1 practice quiz by Friday at 6 PM" ✓

## Study Strategies

### Active Recall > Passive Review
- Passive: re-reading, highlighting → LOW retention
- Active: practice tests, flashcards, teach it → HIGH retention
- Testing effect: retrieving information strengthens memory

### Spaced Repetition
- Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 2 weeks
- Use Anki or physical flashcards
- Harder cards appear more frequently

### Simple Budget Framework (50/30/20)
- 50% Needs (rent, food, transport)
- 30% Wants (dining out, entertainment)
- 20% Savings/Debt repayment
