# BUSN-110-02: Business Bootcamp — Student Success
**Longwood University | Fall 2026**

## Course Description
A practical orientation to college and professional success for business students. Covers academic skills, career development, professional communication, financial literacy, and business fundamentals. Designed to build the habits and skills needed to succeed at Longwood and beyond.

## Learning Objectives
1. Develop effective time management and study strategies
2. Build professional communication skills (written and verbal)
3. Understand basic business concepts and vocabulary
4. Create a career development plan
5. Navigate campus resources effectively

## Key Topics
- Time management: calendars, planners, priority matrices
- Study strategies: active recall, spaced repetition, Cornell notes
- Campus resources: tutoring, advising, career center, library
- Professional email etiquette and LinkedIn fundamentals
- Resume writing and interview preparation
- Financial literacy: budgeting, student loans, credit basics
- Business vocabulary and industry overview
- Networking and professional relationships
- Goal setting: SMART goals
