## Course Description
Survey of the legal framework governing business operations in the United States. Topics include the American legal system, tort law, contract law, business organizations, agency law, employment law, intellectual property, and regulatory compliance.

## Learning Objectives
1. Identify the sources and structure of American business law
2. Apply tort doctrine to business liability scenarios
3. Analyze contract formation, performance, and breach
4. Distinguish between business organization forms and their legal implications
5. Recognize employment law obligations
6. Evaluate intellectual property protections for businesses

## Key Topics
- Constitutional foundations of business regulation
- Torts: negligence, intentional torts, strict liability
- Contract law: formation, performance, breach, remedies
- UCC Article 2 (goods) vs. common law (services)
- Business entities: sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, corporation
- Agency law: fiduciary duties
- Employment and labor law
- Intellectual property: patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets
