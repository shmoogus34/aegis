## Key Vocabulary Blitz
- **Respondeat superior**: Employer liable for employee's torts within scope of employment
- **Vicarious liability**: Liability without direct fault; imputed from another
- **Contributory vs. Comparative negligence**: Contributory = all-or-nothing; comparative = proportional
- **Statute of limitations**: Deadline to file a lawsuit
- **Injunction**: Court order to do/stop doing something
- **Compensatory damages**: Make the plaintiff whole
- **Punitive damages**: Punish extreme misconduct (rare)
- **Liquidated damages**: Pre-agreed damage amount in contract

## Last-Minute Tips
- Read every question twice — trick questions often hinge on "which of the following is NOT..."
- For fact patterns: identify the legal issue first, then apply the rule
- Strict liability = NO need to prove fault — eliminate negligence answer choices
- UCC applies to goods; common law applies to services
