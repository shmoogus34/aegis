Patents
Federal Law – There are NO state patent laws. 
Exclusive right of an inventor to use, sell,  reproduce a discovery or device for 20 years.
A patent protects an idea. To be patented, an invention must be useful, novel, and non-obvious.  
The invention does NOT have to be mechanical. Ex. The bacterium that eats oil slicks is patented. 
Copyright
Registration is permissive and NOT mandatory. It is the only type of intellectual property that does NOT require registration. 
The Mickey Mouse Copyright Term Extension Act added 20 years to the length of copyright protection. The Copyright of an individual lasts for the life of the creator plus 70 years. 
After a copyright ends, then anyone may publish the work.

Crimes
Violation of a public duty and is prosecuted by the state.  The victim of a crime is called the complainant…not the plaintiff.
Which is more serious…a felony or a misdemeanor?
Conspiracy is an agreement to commit a crime – withdrawal is NOT a defense.
Intoxication is not a defense!
Burden of Proof – beyond a reasonable doubt
Larceny and Shoplifting
Larceny is the taking and carrying away the personal property of another with the intent to permanently deprive the owner.
Shoplifting is a statutory form of larceny.  You do not have to leave the store to be convicted of shoplifting.
Robbery
Robbery is the unlawful taking of personal property from the possession or presence of another by means of force or putting the possessor in fear. 
Burglary
“Burglary” at common law was defined as the breaking and entering in the nighttime the dwelling of another with the intent to commit a felony. This definition has been modified by statute.  http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burglary
A person has broken into a house if the door is unlocked and they just walked in.
It occurs if a window is open and a person crawls through.
RICO
If property is used in the commission of a crime OR if property is purchased with money from criminal activities, it may be seized (forfeited) under RICO.
$25,000 fine per violation and/or up to 20 years in prison.
Foreign Corrupt Practices Act
Sui generis – a US law applied to US citizens and businesses doing business abroad.  
Two fold approach 
Accounting Provisions
Anti-bribery Provisions- Sections 103 and 104 prohibit a company from giving anything of value to a foreign official, political party, or political candidate, to obtain or retain business.  Grease payments are specifically excluded.  In other words, grease payments are legal.

Forgery -- Signing another person’s name with the intent to defraud.
Perjury – knowingly giving false testimony after being sworn to tell the truth in a judicial proceeding.  Perjury doe not have to occur a courtroom – for example, it could occur in a deposition taken in a lawyer’s office.  
Embezzlement

Constitution and Crimes
5th Amendment (Double jeopardy) – you can’t be tried twice for the same crime based on the same set of facts.  
Can you be tried for a crime and found not guilty and then be sued civilly? Yes
Fourth Amendment
4th Amendment – Freedom from unreasonable searches and seizures applies to individuals as well as to businesses. 
Note: The 5th Amendment right to refrain from self-incrimination only applies to individuals.  
Miranda v. Arizona
If a person is taken into custody without being given Miranda warnings and then confesses, the confession will be excluded from evidence as “fruit of the poisonous tree”.
If the illegal confession is the only evidence of guilt, the confession will still be excluded from trial.

Ziva Jewelry v. Car Wash Headquarters
Salesman had $851,000 worth of jewelry in the trunk.
He gave his keys to the car wash attendant without telling him that the jewelry was in the trunk.
After the car went through the car wash tunnel, a thief stole the car.  The car was later recovered…not the jewelry.  Is the car wash liable?
NO…no knowledge.
What about the spare tire?
Yes…imputed knowledge.
