First Step – Attachment (must know elements of attachment) 
Three requirements are:  
Security agreement

Perfection – 3 Ways
Perfected security interest may occur when the seller has possession of the collateral.
Perfected security interest may exist if a financing statement is filed. What is the purpose of a financing statement?  A financing statement alerts third parties to the existence of a security interest.  In other words, it puts the world on notice that a security interest exists.
Consumer goods – Automatic perfection when consumer goods are sold.  This perfected security interest may be lost if goods are sold to another consumer.  Thus, it is may be wise to file a financing statement.  Remember to look at where the good are used to determine whether they are “consumer goods”.  
Note:  The purpose of a financing statement is to alert third persons that a creditor has a security interest in the collateral.

Involuntary Bankruptcy
Begun by the creditors of the debtor.  If there are 12 or more creditors, at least 3 must sign the petition.

Discharge in Bankruptcy
Can only obtain a Chapter 7 discharge once within 8 years of filing the current petition.
Releases bankrupt from most debts including debts for breach of contract and negligence.
Certain debts are non-dischargeable: Taxes (within 3 years), unscheduled debts, alimony, child support, fraud or embezzlement, intentional injuries to persons or property,  education loans (unless undue hardship), government fines or penalties (within 3 years), DUI, certain consumer debts, Sabanes-Oxley liability.  
A discharge may be denied if the debtor refuses to obey a lawful order of the court.

Consideration
‘Consideration’ is the price paid for a promise. You must know all of the items listed below.
As a general rule, when there is no consideration for a promise, the agreement is NOT BINDING.
Promise to perform an existing obligation is not consideration. (Ex. Cabin and bonus)
Forbearance (giving up a right to do something) is consideration (Ex. Hamer v. Sidway).  What if I promise to pay my 18 year old son $10,000 if he does not drink alcohol until he is 21.  Is that forbearance? 
Some enforceable obligations do not have to be supported by consideration to be binding – formal contracts (contracts under seal, negotiable instruments, contracts recorded in court) , charitable contributions.
