Other Laws
Equal Pay Act – gender-based wage discrimination is illegal.
Age Discrimination in Employment Act – protects people over the age of 40.  For a willful violation of this act, the plaintiff may recover double damages.
ADA  applies to private employers when they have 15 or more employees.
Sole or Individual Proprietorship
Creature of Common Law
Biggest disadvantage is unlimited personal liability
Partnerships
Written partnership agreement is not required.
Unless otherwise agreed, partners will split profits equally.
Note: The sharing of gross receipts is NOT evidence of a partnership!
Death of a partner automatically causes dissolution by operation of law
Limited Partnership
Joint venture – negligence of one venturer may be imputed on a co-venturer
Corporations
Minimum # of shareholders and board of directors
Advantages: Corporate shield from personal liability, perpetual existence, ease of transfer of ownership.
Disadvantage: Double taxation of profits paid out as dividends.
Death of the owner has no legal effect – perpetual existence
S-Corp – Combines the advantage of the  corporate shield from personal liability (as with a C Corp) and does not have double taxation (an advantage of partnerships).
Cumulative voting is the voting method most likely to give a minority shareholder the opportunity to influence the selection of a member of the board of directors.
Equal Employment Law
Employment at will – If your contract of employment is of uncertain duration, then you may be an employee at will.  This means that you may be fired for any reason or no reason as long as it is not a violation of a law, or in Virginia, an established public policy.
EEOC – is the federal agency that administers equal employment laws
Title VII
Race, color, religion, sex, national origin
Disparate treatment
Disparate impact
Never a bfoq for race or color 
Sexual Harassment – quid pro quo (supervisors seek sexual favors in return for job benefits) and hostile work environment 
Agency and Respondeat Superior
An employer is liable for the negligent torts of an employee if committed within the scope of the employment
Intentional Torts – an employer is only liable for the intentional torts of an employee is committed within the scope of the employment AND in furtherance of the employer’s business
Moritz v. Pines Hotel
