TEST 1 PRACTICE QUIZ KEY
 Communication of the offer from the offeror to the offeree is mandatory.  Keeping this principle in mind… if you find a lost dog and do not know that a reward is being offered, then you ___cannot______ (can or cannot) force the owner to pay the reward.
An express contract is one that is shown through language.
An implied contract (Ex. Going to the doctor) is implied or deduced from the conduct of the parties.  It does not have to be in writing.  
Ambiguous contract terms will be strictly construed against the drafting party – the person who wrote the contract.
The Bill of Rights includes the first 10 Amendments to the Constitution.
If a contract has a liquidated damages clause, the parties agree in advance to the damages in the event of a breach of contract.
A contract made under extreme duress (Ex. At gun point) is void.
May a thief ever convey valid title to goods?  NO…not even to a bona fide purchaser.
According to the Statute of Frauds certain contracts must be in writing and signed by the party to be charged (being sued).
A promise that is not supported by consideration may still be enforced if the doctrine of promissory estoppel can be applied.
Duress is a wrongful threat and creates a voidable agreement.
The General District Court is Virginia’s version of small claims court.
Scienter is the intent to deceive and is an element of “fraud”.
When an acceptance adds an additional term to an offer, it is a counteroffer and under the common law, a counteroffer revokes an offer.
A reward offered to the public for the return of lost property is an offer to make a unilateral contract.
A price quoted in a newspaper ad or catalog is an invitation to negotiate.
Minors are liable for “necessaries” (food, clothing, shelter).  By statute in a majority of states, a minor may also be liable for the following contracts: medical care, education loans, and contracts made while running a business.
If an auction is without reserve, the items auctioned must be sold to the highest bidder.
The rule that a court decision becomes precedent to be followed in later cases is stare decisis (to stand by the decision).
A mutual mistake of fact makes a contract void.  Example:  Atocha case
Consideration (price paid for a promise) is a necessary element of a binding contract; however, it is not required if the document is a formal contract (in Virginia) or for a promise to donate money to a charity.
The main thrust of quasi contract is to avoid unjust enrichment.
A contract in which both parties have fully performed their obligation is an executed contract.
When may a court award specific performance? Real estate contracts, and if the subject matter is unique (antiques, artwork, items of special sentimental value). 
Three types of monetary damages are: nominal ($1.00), compensatory (intended to place the non-breaching party in the position he would have been in without the breach), and punitive (rare). 
A merchant’s firm offer is one that is in writing and signed by the merchant.  It is irrevocable for the period of time stated in the offer…and cannot exceed 3 months.
Illegal contracts are void and unenforceable. 
What are the 3 branches of government? Executive, judicial, and legislative.
After a person has been declared incompetent, all of his/her contracts are void. 
The implied warranty of fitness for a particular purpose is created when the buyer relies on the seller (not necessary to be a merchant) to pick out goods that the buyer requires to meet a stated need.  
The implied warranty of merchantability is made when a merchant is selling goods that he/she ordinarily sells and warrants that the goods are good for the ordinary purpose for which they are used.  This warranty may be disclaimed if the good are sold as is or with all faults or when the seller uses a conspicuous disclaimer that includes the word merchantability.
In a sale on approval, the risk of loss prior to approval or the expiration of the approval period is on the seller.
A contract involving both services and goods is classified as a contract for the sale of goods if the sale of goods is the dominant reason for the contract.
