Duress
Wrongful Threat 
Ordinary Duress – agreement is voidable.  The threat is viewed through the eyes of the threatened party.
Extreme duress – voids an agreement
Mailbox Rule
An acceptance is effective when it is mailed.
Exception:
Offer states that the acceptance is not effective until it is received. 
Acceptance
An effective acceptance must be unequivocal.  It must “mirror” the offer.
If acceptance adds a new term, it is a “counteroffer”.
A counteroffer, revokes an offer.
Example:  I offer to sell my house to you for $50,000.  You decline my offer and offer to buy my house for $45,000.
Note: A mere inquiry is not a counteroffer.  Would you be willing to accept less than $50,000 for your house?
CAPACITY -- MINORS
Minors lack capacity to contract; thus, their contracts are voidable at their election
General Rule: Minors must pay for the reasonable value of “necessaries” (food, clothing, shelter).
What if a minor misrepresents his age?
Capacity
Mental Incapacity
Can a person who has been legally adjudicated incompetent enter into a valid contract? NO.
 Once a person has been declared by a court to be incompetent, any agreements subsequently entered into are void.   
Is it possible for a person who is mentally ill to have a “lucid interval”? YES.
Intoxication – contracts may be “voidable”.  Test is whether a person knew what they were doing.
Corporations – When a contract is beyond the corporate purpose, it is said to be “ultra vires” or unenforceable.  As a practical matter, most corporate charters have a very broad corporate purpose (all legal business) and thus, this is rare.
Mistakes
Unilateral Mistake – generally does not affect the validity of a contract…unless it is so big that the other party knew or should have known.

Other Defenses
Fraud in the inducement is a misrepresentation of a material fact that is relied upon to the detriment of the relying party.
The agreement is voidable and money damages may be sought.
There must be scienter (intent to deceive). And the misrepresentation must be a statement of fact.
Fraud in the execution creates a void agreement.  This occurs in the form of the agreement.  
Revocation
Offer may be revoked prior to acceptance.  Exception: Option contract/option offer
