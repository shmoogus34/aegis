Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964
EEOC – Title VII, Equal Pay Act, ADEA and the ADA
Title VII applies to employers with 15+ employees and to unions.
Prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, gender, or national origin.  
Two theories of discrimination – disparate treatment and disparate impact.
Disparate Treatment
Exists when an employer treats some individuals less favorably than others because of race, color, religion, sex or national origin.
Motive is a necessary element

Religious Discrimination
(2006-16) Marie Jean Pierre worked as a dishwasher at the Conrad  
When she was hired, she told the hotel that she could not work on Sundays due to her involvement in the Soldiers of Christ Church, a Catholic missionary group that helped the poor.   
In 2015, her boss, kitchen manager George Colon, assigned her to work Sundays anyway.
Co-workers traded shifts with Pierre for several weeks, but Colon ultimately demanded she show up. When she refused, he fired her.
Pierre filed a complaint with the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, which issued her a “right to sue” notice. 
She sued the Conrad, managed by HEI Hotels &amp; Resorts and part of the Hilton collection, in U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida in May 2017.
Federal jury found that the Conrad Hotel in Brickell retaliated against a religious dishwasher by firing her for being unable to work on Sundays, and awarded her $21.5 million.

Bona Fide Occupational Qualification
It is not unlawful to discriminate if there is a BFOQ reasonably necessary to the normal operation of a firm.
There is never a BFOQ for race.
UAW v. Johnson Controls
Other Employment Laws
Equal Pay Act -- Prohibits employers from paying employees of one gender lower wages than the rate paid employees of the opposite gender.  Glenn v. GM
Age Discrimination in Employment Act – applies to employers with 20 or more employees and forbids discrimination against people over 40 years of age.  An aggrieved person may get double damages for a willful violation.
