Contractual Liability
An agent takes on the contractual capacity of the principal.  Quaere: Can a minor act as an agent?
An agent is not liable on contracts made within the scope of his authority on behalf of a disclosed principal.
An agent is liable on all contracts made on behalf of an undisclosed principal.  
Torts
Negligent Torts – are committed when there is a failure to act as a reasonable person would act under the circumstances
Intentional Torts – Battery, assault, defamation (libel and slander)
Respondeat Superior
An employer is liable for the negligent torts of an employee if committed within the scope of the employment
Intentional Torts – an employer is only liable for the intentional torts of an employee is committed within the scope of the employments AND in furtherance of the employer’s business.

Termination
Act of the parties.  Notice must be given to those who dealt with or knew of the agency relationship to prevent “apparent authority”
Operation of law – death, insanity, bankruptcy, impossibility.  Notice to third parties is not necessary.
Apparent Authority
A principal may be liable for contracts made by an agent with apparent authority.
Ex. Martha Christiansen owned a clothing store in Lake Placid, NY.  Martha told the store manager, Jane Farley, not to buy blue jeans.  Jane ordered several upscale blue jean outfits from Women’s wear.  Martha refused to pay for them alleging that the manager did not have authority to order blue jeans for the store. Fiduciary Duty
A person acting as an agent owes a fiduciary duty to the principal.  In other words, the agent must act in the best interests of the principal.
Ex. Allen Industries v. Sheldon
K entered into a contract with S to sell a building.  If S sold the building by herself, she would receive the entire commission. 
S presented K with a written offer for $335,000 and K countered with $350.000.  The contract was signed.
S did not tell K that there was an oral offer for more than $350,000 because S would have to split the commission with a cooperating broker.
Did S breach her fiduciary duty?  YES
