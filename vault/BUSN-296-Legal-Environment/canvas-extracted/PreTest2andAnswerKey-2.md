A. Forms of Business Organizations (KEY)
1. What is the minimum number of shareholders that a corporation must have?   ___1__
2. Are sole proprietorships taxed like a corporation?  _No
3. The biggest disadvantage of a sole proprietorship is unlimited personal liability.
4. Does the law require a written partnership agreement?  __No, a partnership is a creature of common law.
5. Name 3 advantages of a corporation.
Perpetual existence
Corporate shield from personal liability_
Easy to transfer ownership interests.  
6. What is the biggest disadvantage of a C Corp?  ____Double taxation of profits paid out as dividends_
7. How does an S Corp combine the advantages of a C Corp and a partnership? With an S Corp, there is no double taxation of profits paid out as dividends (as in a partnership) and the corporate shield from personal liability (as in a C Corp).
8. Does a limited partnership need to have at least one general partner? Yes
9. Bob and Al are partners in a general partnership.  They do not have a written partnership agreement.  A contributes $20,000 in initial capital and Bob contributes $40,000.  How will they split profits? Equally
10. Under which form of business (sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation) would the death of the owner have no legal effect?  Corporation
11. The Southside Eye Center, Inc., a corporation formed in Virginia, has 2 shareholders.  What is the minimum number of people who must serve on the board of directors? 2
12. Cumulative voting is the voting method that is most likely to give a minority shareholder the opportunity to elect a member to the board of directors.
13. A franchise is an arrangement in which the owner of a trademark, tradename, or copyright licenses others, under specified conditions or limitations, to use the trademark, tradename, or copyright in purveying goods or services.
B.  Employment Laws and Agency (KEY)
1.  Unlike a disparate impact case, in a discrimination case based on disparate treatment, the plaintiff is required to prove motive and this may be difficult.   
2.  The Age Discrimination in Employment Act forbids discrimination against persons over 40 years of age.
3.  In addition to quid pro quo (this for that) sexual harassment, the law also forbids hostile work environment harassment.  
4. Race or color can never be a bona fide occupational qualification.  
5.  A written agency agreement is called a power of attorney.
6.  The term apparent authority is used to describe a situation where a principal has created the appearance of authority, but actual authority does not exist.    
7.  The EEOC is the federal agency that administers Title VII, the Equal Pay Act, the Age Discrimination and Employment Act and the Americans with Disabilities Act.  
8. Title VII seeks to eliminate employer and union practices that discriminate against employees and job applicants on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.  
9. When an employer discriminates against a protected class and treats some employees less favorably than others, the legal theory that applies is disparate treatment. 
10.  Title VII protects members of which racial group? All racial groups.
11.   When supervisors seek sexual favors in return for job benefits, the practice is called
quid pro quo sexual harassment.
12. The Equal Pay Act prohibits gender-based wage discrimination.
13.  The ADA applies places of public accommodation, public transportation, telecommunications, and to private employers when they have at least 15 employees.
