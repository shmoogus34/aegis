Parties to a Crime
Parties to a crime – As a rule, in Virginia, everyone who takes part in the planning, execution, or cover-up may be convicted of that crime.  
Responsibility for criminal acts – an employer is liable for the crime of an employee when the employer directs the employee to commit the crime.  
Is the employee also guilty? Yes
May a corporation be held guilty?  Yes.  
May the CEO be prosecuted?  Yes.  (US v. Park)
Attempts and Conspiracies
Attempts – something less than the crime but more than mere preparation. For instance, if Mary tries to poison Tom and fails, then she has the requisite intent and will be charged with attempted murder.
Conspiracy – an agreement between two people to commit a crime.  Withdrawal is not a defense and it does not matter that the crime was not completed or thwarted.
