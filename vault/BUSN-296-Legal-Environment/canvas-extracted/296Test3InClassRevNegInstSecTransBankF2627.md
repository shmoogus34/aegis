Commercial Paper
Types of negotiable instruments – drafts and promissory notes.  A check is a special type of a draft (always drawn on a bank).  A CD is a type of a promissory note.
Characteristics of commercial paper – writing in some permanent, movable form, containing an unconditional promise or order to pay a sum certain in cash.  Note:  While “Pay to the order of…”is required for drafts and notes, a check may say “pay to”.
Order Paper and Bearer Paper
Be able to identify what type of paper you have…order or bearer.
Order paper is payable to a specific person.
Bearer paper is created when an instrument is payable to Cash or Bearer or by an endorsement in blank (the holder just signs their name).		
Order paper is negotiated by endorsement and delivery.
Bearer paper is negotiated by mere delivery.
What if there is a mistake…payee’s name is misspelled, words v. numbers?

Secured Transactions
There are 2 steps in creating a secured transaction -- attachment and perfection.
Step 1 -- Attachment 
Three requirements are:  
Security agreement
Value must be given
Debtor must have rights in the collateral
Step 2: Perfected Security Interest
You must know the methods that a creditor can use to create a perfected security interest.  
Perfection gives a creditor the best possible rights in the collateral
If the debtor declares bankruptcy, a creditor is protected…to the extent that the collateral satisfies the debt
NOTE: If a creditor has a perfected security interest, self-help repossession may be used as long as there is not a breach of the peace.
Perfection Method 1
Perfected security interest may occur when the seller has possession of the collateral.
Note: If the creditor has possession of the collateral, the security agreement may be oral.
What is a common example of the creditor retaining possession of the collateral?
Perfection Method 2
Perfected security interest may exist if a financing statement is filed. 
What is the purpose of a financing statement?  A financing statement alerts third parties to the existence of a security interest.  In other words, it puts the world on notice that a security interest exists.
Perfection Method 3
Consumer goods – Automatic perfection when consumer goods are sold.  This perfected security interest may be lost if goods are sold to another consumer.  Thus, it is may be wise to file a financing statement.  
Remember to look at where the good are used to determine whether they are “consumer goods”.  
Bankruptcy
Chapter 7  (a/k/a Liquidation or Straight Bankruptcy)
Involuntary – begun by the creditors of the debtor 
Voluntary – Begun by the debtor filing a petition.  Note:  A debtor must take a credit counseling course to be able to file for bankruptcy. 
Chapter 13 Extended Time Payment Plan is only available to a debtor who has a regular income. 
Involuntary Bankruptcy
Begun by the creditors of the debtor.  If there are 12 or more creditors, at least 3 must sign the petition.  
You may not force a farmer, persons owing less than $18,600, or a charitable organization into bankruptcy.
NOTE: “Involuntary bankruptcies are primarily filed against businesses, where creditors believe the business can pay its outstanding debts but refuses to do so for some reason. They are less common against individuals because most have few recoverable assets.”
Source: https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/involuntary-bankruptcy.asp

Discharge in Bankruptcy
Can only obtain a Chapter 7 discharge once within   8 years of filing the current petition.
Releases bankrupt from most debts
Certain debts are non-dischargeable: Taxes (within 3 years), unscheduled debts, alimony, child support, fraud or embezzlement, intentional injuries to persons or property,  education loans (unless undue hardship), government fines or penalties (within 3 years), DUI, certain consumer debts, Sabanes-Oxley liability.  
A discharge may be denied if the debtor refuses to obey a lawful order of the court.
