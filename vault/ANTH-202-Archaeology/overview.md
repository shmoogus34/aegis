## Course Description
Introduction to archaeological methods, theory, and practice. Students explore how archaeologists reconstruct past human behavior through the systematic recovery and analysis of material remains, including artifacts, ecofacts, and features.

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the theoretical foundations of archaeology as a discipline
2. Apply systematic field and laboratory methods to archaeological data
3. Analyze material culture in social and historical context
4. Evaluate site preservation ethics and cultural resource management
5. Critically assess archaeological interpretations

## Key Themes
- Archaeological method and theory
- Site survey, excavation, and recording
- Artifact typology and classification
- Dating techniques (relative and absolute)
- Archaeobotany, zooarchaeology, and human osteology
- Cultural resource management (CRM) and preservation ethics
- Anthropological theory applied to material culture
