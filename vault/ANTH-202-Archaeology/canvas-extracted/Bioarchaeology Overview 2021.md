The Skeleton
• 206 Bones
• Skull = 13 Major & 16 Minor Bones
• Post-Cranial Skeleton = 177 Bones
• Cranium = Skull without the mandible
• Calvarium = Cranium w/o Facial Bones
• Dentition = 32 Adult Teeth
• 12 Molars,
• 8 Premolars
• 4 Canines
• 8 Incisors
• Dental Formula = 2-1-2-3
• Post-Cranial Skeleton
• 177 Bones
• 27 Are Single Bones (i.e. vertebrae, & sternum)
• 150 Are Paired: Left & Right
• Long Bones- tubular (>20cm.) - Limbs
• Short Bones – tubular (<10cm.) – Hands, Feet, Clavicle
• Flat Bones – Pelvis, Scaulae, Ribs, Sternum
• Irregular Bones – Vertebrae, Carpals, Tarsals, Patellae

Mortuary Analysis: Burial Orientation &
Placement
• Orientation of burial
• (ex/ E-W orientation of Christian burials)
• Burial Placement
• In formal cemetery
• Under houses
• Outskirts or center of village/city
• Association with other burials or features
• Family/clan groupings
• Areas reserved for individuals of certain social strata
• Burial on/near sacred sites/objects

• Shroud burials

Mortuary Analysis: Grave Goods
• Grave goods- objects buried with the dead
• Can be everyday common-place items, ornate
ceremonial objects, and/or exotic trade goods
• Reflective of an individual’s “place” in society:
• Gender
• Occupation
• Socio-economic status
• Prestige and/or power
