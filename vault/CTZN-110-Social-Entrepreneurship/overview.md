# CTZN-110-58-HONS: Social Entrepreneurship & Global Citizenship
**Longwood University | Fall 2026 | HONORS SECTION**

## Course Description
Explores the intersection of entrepreneurial thinking and global civic responsibility. Students examine how social enterprises, NGOs, and impact-driven businesses address complex global challenges. The honors section requires deeper engagement with primary sources and an original social venture proposal.

## Learning Objectives
1. Analyze social entrepreneurship models and their theoretical foundations
2. Apply design thinking to social problems
3. Evaluate impact measurement frameworks (Theory of Change, Social ROI)
4. Engage with global citizenship concepts and SDGs
5. Develop and present an original social venture concept

## Key Topics
- Social entrepreneurship vs. traditional entrepreneurship
- Business models for social impact: B-Corp, CIC, nonprofit, hybrid
- Design thinking process: Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test
- Theory of Change and logic models
- UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Global citizenship: rights, responsibilities, cosmopolitan ethics
- Impact investing and ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance)
- Case studies: TOMS, Grameen Bank, Warby Parker, Khan Academy

## Honors Requirements
- Extended reading list (additional academic articles)
- Social venture proposal (group, 20-page final project)
- Deeper peer discussion engagement
