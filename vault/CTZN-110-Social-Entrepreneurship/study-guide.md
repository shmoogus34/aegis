# CTZN-110 Study Guide — Social Entrepreneurship

## Key Concepts

### Social Entrepreneurship vs. Traditional
| Feature | Traditional Entrepreneur | Social Entrepreneur |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------|
| Primary goal | Profit | Social value creation |
| Success metric | ROI, revenue | Impact, beneficiaries served |
| Revenue | Customer payment | Mixed (earned + donated) |
| Example | Amazon | Grameen Bank |

### Design Thinking Stages (memorize order)
1. **Empathize** → understand users
2. **Define** → frame the problem (HMW...)
3. **Ideate** → brainstorm solutions
4. **Prototype** → build quick mockup
5. **Test** → get feedback, iterate

### Logic Model Flow
Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact

### B-Corp vs. Nonprofit
- **B-Corp**: For-profit, certified by B-Lab, balances profit + purpose
- **Nonprofit**: Tax-exempt, mission-only, no profit distribution

### SDG Quick Reference (first 5)
1. No Poverty
2. Zero Hunger
3. Good Health
4. Quality Education
5. Gender Equality

### Grameen Bank Key Facts
- Founded: 1983, Muhammad Yunus, Bangladesh
- Model: Microloans to women in groups (peer accountability)
- Impact: 9M+ borrowers, 97% repayment rate
- Recognition: Nobel Peace Prize 2006

### Nussbaum Cosmopolitanism
- All humans have equal moral worth regardless of nationality
- Basis for global citizenship obligations
- Counter-argument: Prioritarianism (we owe more to those closest to us)
