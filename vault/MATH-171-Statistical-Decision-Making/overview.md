# MATH-171-01: Statistical Decision Making
**Longwood University | Fall 2026**

## Course Description
Introduction to statistics with an emphasis on decision-making in business and everyday contexts. Topics include descriptive statistics, probability, sampling distributions, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, and linear regression.

## Learning Objectives
1. Calculate and interpret descriptive statistics
2. Apply probability rules to real scenarios
3. Construct and interpret confidence intervals
4. Perform and interpret hypothesis tests
5. Fit and interpret simple linear regression models
6. Communicate statistical results clearly

## Key Topics
- Data types and levels of measurement
- Frequency distributions, histograms, boxplots
- Mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation
- Probability fundamentals: addition, multiplication rules
- Probability distributions: Binomial, Normal
- Central Limit Theorem
- Confidence intervals (proportions, means)
- Hypothesis testing (one-sample, two-sample t-tests, chi-square)
- Simple linear regression and correlation
